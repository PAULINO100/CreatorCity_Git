# Atlas City: Districts Guide

## Overview
Districts are themed zones within Atlas City. Each district has unique entry requirements, visual identity, and bonus multipliers.

## The 5 Districts

### 🔵 Tech District
- **Color**: Blue (#3b82f6)
- **Requirement**: Score ≥ 5,000
- **Bonus**: +10% Influence Score
- **Profile**: Backend architects, system designers, framework creators
- **Phenotype**: Tall sleek towers with blue glow effects

### 🟣 Creator District
- **Color**: Purple (#a855f7)
- **Requirement**: Score ≥ 3,000
- **Bonus**: +15% Visibility
- **Profile**: Designers, content creators, visual storytellers
- **Phenotype**: Artistic structures with purple illumination

### 🟢 Science District
- **Color**: Green (#22c55e)
- **Requirement**: Score ≥ 7,000
- **Bonus**: +20% Credibility
- **Profile**: Data scientists, ML engineers, researchers
- **Phenotype**: Laboratory-style buildings with green bioluminescence

### 🟡 Education District
- **Color**: Yellow (#eab308)
- **Requirement**: Score ≥ 2,000
- **Bonus**: +12% Reach
- **Profile**: Teachers, tutorial creators, documentation champions
- **Phenotype**: Open campus buildings with warm golden light

### 🟠 Startup District
- **Color**: Orange (#f97316)
- **Requirement**: Score ≥ 4,000
- **Bonus**: +18% Growth
- **Profile**: Founders, product builders, growth hackers
- **Phenotype**: Compact garages with energetic orange pulses

## Rules
1. Each citizen may claim **exactly one** district
2. Claims are **permanent** (no transfers in Phase 2)
3. District bonuses are applied to the citizen's public profile score
4. The claim button is only available if the citizen meets the minimum score requirement

## Expansion Roadmap
- **Phase 3**: District governance (voting on district policies)
- **Phase 4**: Inter-district trade (City Credit marketplace)
- **Phase 5**: Custom districts (community-created)
